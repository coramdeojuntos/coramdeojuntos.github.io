Coram Deo Juntos

Coram Deo means “before the face of God,” and juntos means “together.” Here you’ll find Bible studies, series and tools for knowing God and living every day before him, together.

Studies and series

  1. Study series · 7 studies · In SpanishGospel-Centered ChristianityBible studies in plain Spanish for understanding the gospel, resting in God’s grace and growing as disciples of Jesus. One companion study is also in English.If Grace Is Free, Why Change? →
  2. Discipleship tool · Spanish and EnglishThe Gospel TimelineA map of the Christian life on one page, from before time to glory, with a walk-through guide that prints every Bible passage in full.
  3. Study series · Lectio Divina · Spanish and EnglishExperiencing God TogetherLectio Divina for reading the Bible slowly, in silence and in community: five passages a week, in three translations, with questions for the group. All three series, on God’s attributes, salvation and Advent, in Spanish and English.
  4. Teaching series · 8 teachings · In SpanishEtapas del camino con JesúsStages of the journey with Jesus: a pastoral map for recognizing how Jesus forms us through life, in our choices and practices, and also through limits, losses, questions and wounds.
  5. Devotional commentary · Spanish and EnglishThe Gospel of MarkA devotional commentary through all of Mark in twenty-five passages, written to be read slowly with an open Bible, from the first announcement of good news to the empty tomb.
  6. Daily readings · Spanish and EnglishFrom Holy Week to PentecostThe four Gospels as one story: ten daily readings for Jesus's last week and four for the weeks after the resurrection, through Pentecost, with dates for each reading and a printable PDF.
