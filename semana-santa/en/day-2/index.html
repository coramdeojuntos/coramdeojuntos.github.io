Sabbath in Bethany

It’s the Sabbath, the Jewish day of rest from Friday sundown to Saturday sundown, so Jesus stays in Bethany. John doesn’t name the day of the dinner given in his honor; many think it was Saturday evening, once the Sabbath had ended.

In this reading

  1. Mary anoints Jesus
  2. Crowds come to see Lazarus

Mary anoints Jesus

John 12:2–8

2A dinner was prepared in Jesus’ honor. Martha served, and Lazarus was among those who ate with him. 3Then Mary took a twelve-ounce jar of expensive perfume made from essence of nard, and she anointed Jesus’ feet with it, wiping his feet with her hair. The house was filled with the fragrance.

4But Judas Iscariot, the disciple who would soon betray him, said, 5“That perfume was worth a year’s wages. It should have been sold and the money given to the poor.” 6Not that he cared for the poor—he was a thief, and since he was in charge of the disciples’ money, he often stole some for himself.

7Jesus replied, “Leave her alone. She did this in preparation for my burial. 8You will always have the poor among you, but you will not always have me.”

Also inMatthew 26:6–13; Mark 14:3–9

NoteMatthew and Mark tell this story later in the week, just before Judas goes to the leading priests; they name the host as Simon and say she poured the perfume on Jesus’s head. Many harmonies see one dinner, told by different witnesses.

Crowds come to see Lazarus

John 12:9–11

9When all the people heard of Jesus’ arrival, they flocked to see him and also to see Lazarus, the man Jesus had raised from the dead. 10Then the leading priests decided to kill Lazarus, too, 11for it was because of him that many of the people had deserted them and believed in Jesus.
